A summary is a decision about what matters. When the TL;DR button makes that decision for a post of mine, I want to know how.

## The prompt

Three sentences, plain text, no headings, no bullet points, written for someone deciding whether to read the whole post. The model gets the post text and nothing else: no title, no tags, so the summary has to come from what the post says.

## What tends to go

Caveats. A post that says "this works on Linux, probably on macOS, and I have not tried Windows" becomes "this works". Numbers survive better than qualifiers. Jokes go first, which is fine.

## What I do about it

I write the first paragraph as if it were the summary. When the TL;DR button and my first paragraph disagree, one of them is wrong, and it is usually the paragraph.

## Turning it off

Short posts do not need a summary of themselves. Set `ai: { tldr: false }` and the button does not render.
