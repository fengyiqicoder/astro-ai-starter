Accessibility is usually discussed as contrast, keyboard access and screen readers. Reading level belongs on the list too.

## The Plain English button

It rewrites the post with shorter sentences and common words, keeps every fact and every code block, and marks the result so you know it is a rewrite. The original is one click away.

## Who it is for

Someone reading in their second language. Someone new to the field who does not yet have the vocabulary. Someone at the end of a long day. Which is most of us, some of the time.

## What it does not do

It does not add explanations the post did not contain. If a term is used without a definition, it stays undefined; select it and press Explain if you want one. Rewriting and explaining are different jobs and the buttons keep them apart.
