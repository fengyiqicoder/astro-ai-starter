Most blogs that offer translations do it one of two ways: a separate set of routes per language, or a link to an external translator. The first means maintaining copies. The second sends your readers away.

## In place

Press Translate, pick a language, and the post rewrites itself paragraph by paragraph. Headings stay headings, lists stay lists, code blocks are left exactly as they were. The original comes back with one click.

## Which language first

The menu lists the languages in `site.ai.languages` and puts the reader's browser language first when it is one of them. Change the list to the languages your readers actually have.

## Cost

A 1,200-word post is roughly 1,600 tokens in and 1,800 out, so a translation costs about 3,400 tokens from the site's allowance. The free allowance covers a few hundred of them. Readers who translate the same post twice in a session get the cached result.

## When not to use it

If you already maintain real translations, turn the button off for those posts with `ai: { translate: false }`. A human translation of your own writing is better than an on-the-fly one, and the starter should not get in its way.
